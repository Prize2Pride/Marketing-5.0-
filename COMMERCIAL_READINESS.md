# Commercial Readiness and Subscription Requirements

Prize2Pride can present teachers and learners with clear, outcome-based plans once a supported payment provider is configured. No price, discount guarantee, competitor-price promise, or lifetime-free commitment is published in the product until it is approved by the platform owner and backed by enforceable billing terms.

| Plan concept | Intended access | Billing prerequisites |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Learner | Enrollments, progress, Creator Studio, reviewed course blueprints | Entitlement mapping, renewal/cancellation rules, invoice and tax requirements. |
| Educator | Teacher subplatform, classes, resources, published-source transformations, year-limited recordings | Educator verification, storage quota policy, retention disclosure, support terms. |
| School / organization | Multiple educators, shared governance, reporting, negotiated capacity | Organization roles, data-processing agreement, administrator controls, service-level terms. |

## Required Billing Controls

Before any plan is purchasable, the owner must approve the payment provider, supported currencies, tax treatment, refund/cancellation policy, invoice requirements, credit consumption rules for AI generation, storage limits, and the exact retention disclosures for recordings. Payment success must create a server-verified entitlement; the client must never grant access solely from a redirect or front-end state.

## AI and Media Credits

If the platform later sells generation credits, each chargeable operation must publish its unit definition, limits, expiry rules, failure/refund handling, and a user-visible usage history. Image, document, and future audio/video generation must remain subject to consent, rights review, and the existing artifact-review workflow where the output is used in a shared course.

> This document is an operational readiness specification, not an offer for sale. Pricing and payment activation remain intentionally disabled until an approved payment integration is configured.
