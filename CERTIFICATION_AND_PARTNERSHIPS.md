# Certification and Partnership Readiness

Prize2Pride can issue meaningful learning certificates only after the owner defines the required evidence for each subject school, such as completed lessons, scored assessments, reviewed practical work, and any educator verification. A certificate should identify the learner, subject school, achievement criteria, issuance date, a verifiable identifier, and the issuer. It must not imply external accreditation unless an accrediting body formally grants it.

| Track | Readiness now | Activation requirement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Learner certificates | Course progress, projects, quiz artifacts, and educator review foundations exist | Certificate templates, eligibility rules, immutable verification IDs, and owner-approved issuance policy. |
| Educator certificates | Educator profile review and school ownership exist | Teaching-evidence rubric, educator review policy, and credential suspension/revocation process. |
| Agency partnerships | School / organization model and governed content workflow exist | Written partner terms, data-processing controls, named administrators, and service scope. |
| API access | Internal server procedures are established but not public APIs | OAuth/scoped credentials, rate limits, audit logging, versioning, abuse controls, and a published developer agreement. |

## Payment Dependency

Paid certificates, verification fees, credit bundles, and agency billing remain disabled until the owner provides a supported payment configuration. Their policy and entitlement rules can be finalized independently, but no paid access is promised or activated without server-verified billing.
