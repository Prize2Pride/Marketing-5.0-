# Prize2Pride Creative Learning Studio Standard

The Studio transforms a teacher-owned lesson or reviewed course artifact into a **reviewable production brief**. It does not claim that a PDF, music track, chart, poster, infographic, or video has been rendered unless the corresponding export or generation result is actually available.

| Output | Required production elements | Quality gate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Course / document | Reader promise, logical hierarchy, headings, learning objective, practice, knowledge check, references or assumptions | The author reviews content and may export DOCX; a print-ready preview is distinct from a finished PDF. |
| Chart / infographic / poster | Audience, single key message, data or evidence assumptions, hierarchy, visual direction, alt text | No quantitative claim is treated as evidence unless the source is supplied. |
| Audio / music brief | Learning goal, duration, structure, narration or lyric-safe concept, rights-safe sound direction, transcript plan | A brief is not an audio recording; protected lyrics and unlicensed music are excluded. |
| Video tutorial | Hook, scene timeline, narration, visual direction, on-screen text, captions, end action | A storyboard is not a rendered video; visual claims and demonstrations must be reviewable. |

Every output uses the author’s selected language—English, French, or Arabic/Darja—and includes a short publish checklist: audience fit, spelling and grammar review, factual verification, accessibility review, rights check, and teacher approval.
