# Prize2Pride v2.0: Expanded Curriculum Outline

## Goal
To establish Prize2Pride as the definitive, AI-augmented education platform for Digital Marketing 5.0, AI Automation, and E-commerce, surpassing existing offerings through comprehensive, multilingual, and AI-powered content.

## Core Pillars of the Expanded Curriculum

1.  **Digital Marketing 5.0 Mastery:** Deep dive into advanced strategies, attention engineering, loyalty, and agentic marketing.
2.  **AI Automation & Claude Mastery:** Comprehensive understanding and practical application of AI agents, automation tools, and large language models (LLMs) like Claude.
3.  **E-commerce & Autonomous Commerce:** Building, scaling, and automating e-commerce operations from foundational principles to mega-scale autonomous systems.
4.  **AI & AGI/ASI Principles in Marketing:** Theoretical and practical understanding of advanced AI concepts and their application in future marketing landscapes.
